Security and vulnerability disclosure

How to report

Email cto@sayanjalinexus.com with the affected repository or page, a concise description, reproduction steps, potential impact and a safe contact method.

Scope

This channel is intended for security issues in publicly maintained SAYANJALI NEXUS websites and repositories. Do not access, alter or retain another person’s data, disrupt services or test systems without permission.

What to include

  • Affected URL, repository and version or commit where known.
  • Steps to reproduce and the expected versus observed behaviour.
  • Impact assessment and relevant logs or screenshots with secrets removed.
  • Your preferred contact details and any suggested mitigation.

Programme status

No bug-bounty programme is currently announced.
